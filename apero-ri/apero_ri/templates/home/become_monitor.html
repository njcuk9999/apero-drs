APERO Monitors help keep the instrument data reduction pipelines running smoothly — checking data quality, helping observers and instrument teams, and learning a huge amount about the instruments along the way.

What does a Monitor do?

Monitoring is a great way to get hands-on experience with real observatory data, build your CV, and support the wider community while you're studying. It's flexible, remote, and looks great on a fellowship or job application.
